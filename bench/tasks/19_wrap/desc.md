Greedy word wrap. Split `text` into words on any whitespace. Build lines by adding words, separated by single spaces, as long as the line stays at most `width` characters; when the next word doesn't fit, start a new line. A word longer than `width` is first cut into chunks of exactly `width` characters (the last chunk may be shorter), and the chunks are then treated as separate words. Lengths count characters, not bytes. Text with no words gives an empty list.
